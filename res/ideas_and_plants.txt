### Redo & Undo ###

There's a problem arising in the following scenario:
1.) You move an actor from InstanceTileMap1 to InstanceTileMap2.
    this action keeps track of two of the following variables:
    -Actor* parent
    -int ordering_index
2.) You delete InstanceTileMap1, or in other words 'stash' it, since it's still alive in the action-stack.
3.) You make changes until you start reaching the max size of the stack, making the change removing InstanceTileMap1
    un-undoable. In other words, InstanceTileMap1 is permanently free'd when this happens.

Question: Can I ensure that a move-action like this does not refer to an object that is free'd, like InstanceTileMap1?

### Recursive Copying ###

I have decided that recursive copying would in fact be useful. Currently I need to use ufo::gc::Json objects to convert an actor with all its
components and configurations to get the exact tree structure I want. The properties I would like to copy are
-Default properties
-custom properties, i.e ufo-variables
-components, i.e actors attached to an actor.

All these *might* be easily solvable with some code-generation, the only really controversial one being components.
